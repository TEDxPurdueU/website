What TEDx means

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. Ours is run entirely by Purdue students, under license from TED.

2027 Annual Conference · Theme

Unseen

The work nobody claps for. The data we never collected. The people in the room who were never asked. Unseen is about what sits just outside our attention — and what changes the moment we look.

Date
Spring 2027 — TBA
Venue
Purdue Memorial Union
Tickets

Three questions we're building the day around

01

Who is missing?

Labor, care, and expertise that hold systems up without appearing in them.

02

What can't we measure?

The limits of instruments, models, and the metrics we mistake for reality.

03

What do we refuse to see?

Attention as a choice — and the cost of looking away on purpose.

Student Speaker Competition

One slot on the Unseen stage goes to a student. You don't need a title, a lab, or a startup — you need one idea you can defend in six minutes.

Applications open Fall 2026

How it works

Step 01

Submit a pitch

200 words on your idea and why it matters now.

Step 02

Audition round

Shortlisted students give a three-minute version to our curation team.

Step 03

Coaching

Finalists get four weeks of rehearsal and script work with our speaker coaches.

Step 04

The stage

The winner speaks at the conference, filmed for the TEDx channel.

Who can enter

  • Any currently enrolled Purdue undergraduate or graduate student
  • Any discipline — engineering to art history
  • Ideas only: no product pitches, promotions, or fundraising

What judges look for

  • One clear idea, not a summary of a field
  • Evidence you actually did the thinking
  • A reason this audience should care today
